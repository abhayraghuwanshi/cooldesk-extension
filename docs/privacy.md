# Privacy

_Published at https://cool-desk.com/privacy-details (linked from the winget
listing's Privacy Url and the in-app Settings toggle)._

CoolDesk runs locally. Your tabs, workspaces, notes, history, and searches stay
on your device — we don't have a server that stores them.

## Anonymous usage stats

To know how many people use CoolDesk and whether it's working, the desktop app
sends one small anonymous ping per day (piggybacked on its update check). It
contains only:

- a random install ID (a UUID — not tied to you, your name, or your email)
- your OS (e.g. `windows`), app version, install source, and language (e.g. `en`)
- a count of how many times you opened Spotlight that day

That's it. We **never** collect your search queries, the URLs or apps you open,
your tabs, your files, or your IP address. No third-party analytics or ad
trackers are used.

## Turning it off

Open **Settings → General → Updates → Anonymous Usage Stats** and switch it off.
The app keeps working exactly the same.
